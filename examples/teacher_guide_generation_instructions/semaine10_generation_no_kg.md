# Semaine 10 — Per-week brief

Pair this brief with `teacher_guide_generation_template_curriculum_doc.md` and `reading_wolof_CE1_fixed.docx` to generate the Semaine 10 reading teacher guide. The template is week-agnostic; this brief supplies the curated, week-specific facts that the template references.

---

## 1. Week parameters

- **Week number ({N}):** 10
- **Palier ({P}):** 2 (Jéego 2)
- **Week type:** regular instructional week
- **Position-in-palier descriptor:** first regular instructional week of Palier 2 — the week that introduces descriptive-text work for the first time. Treat as a model-week-level opening guide for the palier.
- **Target genre (per palier framing):** descriptive texts (jukki melool)

### Palier framing bridge (verbatim, for Jour 1)

Because Semaine 10 is the first regular instructional week of Palier 2, place this bridge once, briefly, in Jour 1 — typically in the Expression Orale L1 *mise en situation* or as a short standalone palier note. It marks the genre transition from narrative (Palier 1) to descriptive (Palier 2).

> **Wolof:** Bii ayu-bés, dunu nettali ay xew-xew. Danuy melool ay këfin.
>
> **French:** Cette semaine, nous n'allons pas raconter des événements. Nous allons décrire des objets.

Do not repeat this framing inside every session. Per template §3, omit the bridge entirely for non-first-of-palier weeks (Sem 11–16).

## 4. Brief-supplemented content

Items where `reading_wolof_CE1_fixed.docx` carries a placeholder or generic content; supply the actual Week-10 material here.

### Mots Fréquents L2 word list

The per-week Contenu for this strand in `reading_wolof_CE1_fixed.docx` is the placeholder *"Mots fréquents — Echelle du MOHEBS (A construire)"*. Use this curated batch of 4–6 words for Week 10, prioritizing high-frequency words that double as descriptive vocabulary:

> **grand, petit, rouge, devant, dans, avec**

Substitute or trim if a different MOHEBS-aligned subset becomes available.

### CGP L1/L2 digraph subset

The CGP Contenu in `reading_wolof_CE1_fixed.docx` lists all Palier-2 reinforcement digraphs as a single 7-week scope. For Week 10, pick a small diagnostic subset (4–6 items) appropriate to the class's diagnostic level. Default subset:

> **au, eau, eu, en, an** (with **ai, ei** as a stretch group)

Adjust based on diagnostic outcomes from prior weeks.

### Récitation content type

The per-week Contenu in `reading_wolof_CE1_fixed.docx` for Sem 10–12 is *"Taalif, waxin ak liyaar yu dëppo / Poèmes, expression, intonation et gestes qui conviennent"* — i.e. **poème** (not prose). Use a memorized poem with appropriate expression, intonation, and gestures. Prose recitation begins at Sem 13.

### Vocabulaire L1 — descriptive-production structures

Treat **dafa / dañu / dañoo** and **am na / am nañu** as descriptive-production structures practiced **in context**, not as isolated vocabulary items. They support the production of short descriptive sentences (*Mu dafa réy*, *Yi dañu ñuul*, *Am na ñetti pàcc*).

### Pedagogical constraint — Production d'Écrits W10

Because Week 10 is the first descriptive-text week of Palier 2, **do not require full descriptive compositions**. Stop at identifying descriptive-text features (object/subject described, parts, qualities, function/use, precise vocabulary) and short guided descriptive sentences. Full guided composition begins from Sem 11 onward.

## 5. Required L1↔L2 transfer pairs

Apply the template's §9 transfer protocol with the following pairs. Mark each with **🔁** in the DOCX.

| # | Pair | Transfer activity | Placement | Minimum operationalization |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **sama / sa / -am** ↔ **mon/ma/mes, ton/ta/tes, son/sa/ses** | Guided translation + parallel-structure comparison | Grammaire L2 | Pupils translate at least 4 short noun phrases between the two paradigms |
| 2 | **dégtal / énonciatif sotti weesu** with **-oon** ↔ **imparfait de l'indicatif** | Parallel comparison + bilingual reformulation | Conjugaison L2 | Pupils write or say one verb's paradigm in both languages side by side |
| 3 | L1 possessives ↔ L2 feminine rule | Contrast: how each language marks meaning differently | Orthographe L2 | Pupils identify in writing what each language marks (possession vs. gender) |
| 4 | Vocabulaire L1 (descriptive structures) ↔ Vocabulaire L2 (descriptive vocabulary) | Bilingual reformulation using same familiar object | Vocabulaire L2 — Session 1 or 2 | Pupils describe the same object in Wolof, then in French |
| 5 | Expression Orale L1 (Jour 1) → Expression Orale L2 (Jour 5) | Re-describe the same object in French | Expression Orale L2 | Pupils produce the French version of the L1 description |
| 6 | Compréhension L1 → Compréhension L2 | Reuse mental-image and "what is the text about?" strategies | CA/CE L2 sessions | Pupils explicitly apply the strategy in L2 |

**Minimum operationalization target:** at least **4 of these 6** pairs must include an explicit pupil production step (not just a teacher-stated observation). See template §9.1.

## 7. Error calibration examples

Per template Appendix A, each content-rich session must include a session-specific likely pupil error and a one-line corrective feedback. Use the W10 examples below as the calibration anchor for this week — they show the expected level of granularity. The model can supply additional W10-specific errors at the same level of detail for any session not listed.

| Session | ❌ Too generic | ✅ Specific enough |
|---|---|---|
| Expression Orale | "Student has difficulty describing" | "Student narrates an event instead of describing an object. Correction: *Danuy melool rekk, dunu nettali. Waxal naka mu mel.*" |
| Grammaire L1 (possessives) | "Student confuses possessives" | "Student writes -am as a separate word (*mbubb am*) instead of suffixing it (*mbubbam*). Correction: *-am dafay toftal ci birim baat bi.*" |
| Conjugaison L1 (-oon) | "Student makes conjugation errors" | "Student adds -oon to the verb (*jàngoon*) instead of the pronoun-aspect marker (*dafoon jàng*). Correction: *-oon dafa tëral ci dafa/dama/dañu, du ci waxe bi.*" |
| Vocabulaire L1 (descriptive structures) | "Student doesn't use structures" | "Student uses *dafa* for plural subjects. Correction: *Benn këfin: dafa. Ñaari këfin: dañu.*" |
| Orthographe L2 (féminin) | "Student forgets the rule" | "Student writes *grande* for a masculine noun. Correction: -e is only added for the feminine form." |
| Grammaire L2 (possessifs) | "Student confuses possessives" | "Student uses *mon* before a feminine noun (*mon trousse*). Correction: *mon* = masculin, *ma* = féminin → *ma trousse*. Exception: *mon amie* (before a vowel)." |
