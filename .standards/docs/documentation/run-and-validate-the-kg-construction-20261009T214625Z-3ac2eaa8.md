### LP pipeline page: how checkpoints are checked and saved

- Audience: pipeline operators and backend developers. File:
  `docs/pipeline/learning-progressions.md`, new subsection "How checkpoints are
  checked and saved" under "Concurrency, checkpoints, and failure".
- ACs and criteria: `AC-024`; design LP checkpoint contract items 1, 2, 4 and
  7, and the `AC-024` Technical Acceptance Criterion (full-verification points,
  guards between them, incremental journaled commits with unchanged at-rest
  files).
- Content and the code it was checked against:
  - Full verification and where it runs: store construction (`_load`, or the
    initial `_save` on a fresh start) plus the population verifier's
    constructor; the completion boundary in `_save`; the `full=True` call after
    `_finish_lp_requests`; read-only loads (`validate_lp_checkpoint_format`,
    finalization, eval sampling).
  - Guards: `_check_checkpoint_files(full=False)` and
    `LPRequestPopulationVerifier.guard` (stat identity of five fields,
    re-read on any difference, `INFO` log on identical bytes, fail closed on
    different bytes); per-request re-hash for dispatch, transport (all active
    requests) and publication (the published request plus those in flight);
    execution-material recheck in `_verify_lp_inputs`.
  - Coarse-timestamp limit stated as "detected no later than the next full
    verification", as the implementation review asked.
  - Saves: stage files appended (`_append_bytes`), small files and receipt
    rewritten whole (`_SMALL_NAMES`, `_atomic_write`); byte identity with the
    full-rewrite writer (design item 4; Tester Increment 1 and reviewer R1/R2
    evidence; test `test_every_commit_leaves_the_bytes_a_full_rewrite_would_write`
    and `test_directory_left_by_the_pre_change_writer_loads_and_resumes`).
  - Journal: fields written by `_save`; apply, check, delete order in
    `_commit_delta`; roll forward only from exact previous or next states in
    `_recover_delta`; old full-payload journals still recovered in `_recover`.
  - Torn append after an OS crash or power loss fails closed and means LP
    regeneration (implementation review limit).
  - Run time limited mainly by model time (design Decision 9; reviewer R10:
    lowest window at 78.7% of its model-bound rate); `max_concurrent_requests`
    is part of `lp_execution_material`, so changing it means LP regeneration.
- Also corrected in the same page: "The six example profiles" became "Every
  example profile under `examples/`". All seven example configs set
  `unresolved_participation` to `include_unresolved_with_warnings` (grep of
  `examples/`). The count went stale when the previous cycle added the CAPS
  `kgs` block; the new wording does not depend on a count.
